learn about more color theory

Color theory is the art and science of how colors interact, how humans perceive them, and how they combine to create visual harmony, contrast, and emotional impact in design and art. It uses tools like the color wheel to guide choices in branding, fashion, and painting.

the color wheel

The color wheel is a visual representation of colors arranged in a circle, showing the relationships between different hues. It helps designers understand how colors can be combined to create harmonious palettes or striking contrasts.
